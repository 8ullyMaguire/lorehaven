# 25. The risk register

**Status:** reserved
**Milestone:** —
**Source:** the corresponding section of the Darkhold specification

| # | risk | likelihood | impact | mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R-1** | **The spec/ledger rot.** The archive's own status claims drift from the code, and the next reader trusts them | **high** — five times in lorehaven, once in kindred | high | re-derive the ledger; put the command in every status document; `verified_at` is the only authority (§23.1) |
| **R-2** | **The three-way vocabulary mismatch leaks into the code** | high | medium | §5 is normative and the mapping is published; a migration that introduces an unmapped name is a review failure |
| **R-3** | **The 107-site adapter port takes longer than everything else combined** | high | medium | M8 ships 12 first; the families make the long tail cheap; each adapter is independently shippable and independently valuable |
| **R-4** | **Reachability.** AO3 and FFN are blocked from the estate's build host | **certain today** | high | the honest answer is in the UI; **the launch corpus does not need AO3 at all** (§9.8) because the local mirror carries the metadata |
| **R-5** | **The taste service's memory budget is breached by corpus growth nobody forecast** | medium | high | the cap is a hard number, the gate boots the real binary, `/healthz` 503s when over; a lite mode that renormalises its weights rather than dividing by zero |
| **R-6** | **The arena's Glicko-2 port disagrees with kindred's** | medium | high | the port is checked against kindred's implementation on kindred's corpus, case by case, before it is believed |
| **R-7** | **Guests + the fold become a trust farm** | medium | high | `merge.grants_trust` defaults to **false**; the fold grants tenure only (§14.2) |
| **R-8** | **The jury is unreachable** because nobody has reached level 3 | **certain on a new site** | medium | elastic allocation is the design, not a fallback (§14.4, §14.5) |
| **R-9** | **D-9's exposure.** An instance that permits content involving minors, age-gated and labelled, carries real legal and reputational risk if it is indexed or mishandled | **certain** (it is already the case) | **high** | the age gate and the warnings are load-bearing, not advisory; the `robots.txt` is generated and disallows indexing; DMCA and abuse escalation are fast; the owner keeps the deployment choice. **The archive's contribution is the mechanism, not the judgement** |
| **R-10** | **The tier model reads as "paywalling the good algorithm"** | medium | high | the tier is published in `/api/v1/meta`; the downgrade is explained, never silent; the rule that a paying subscriber gets no *rank* is enforced by a mutation test (§23.4) |
| **R-11** | **The house theme drowns an individual reader's taste** | medium | medium | the theme is one term among nine with a decaying weight (§12.2); the diversity floor adds, never removes; a reader's own evidence decays the theme's contribution |
| **R-12** | **A mood becomes a filter by accident** | medium | high | the set-equality mutation test (§23.4); the contract in §12.3 |
| **R-13** | **A cold mood is presented as a confident answer** | medium | high | the cold-mood degradation is a first-class response (`MOOD_COLD`) and a rendered note; silence is a bug |
| **R-14** | **The theme rework weakens the 543 frontend tests instead of re-deriving them** | **high** — this is the estate's most common pattern | medium | §21.2 constraint 4 is explicit; the rework's own gate requires the assertions to be re-derived, not relaxed, and a diff that only loosens an assertion is a review failure |
| **R-15** | **The reader becomes unreadable in a dark theme** | medium | **high** | the reader surface's contrast ratio is a measured gate, not a judgement call; the reader scale stays independent of the site scale |
| **R-16** | **Scope creep into an economy** that turns the archive into a shop | medium | high | §18 is deliberately one entitlement that grants compute; the core path works with no processor configured; no purchase touches rank, trust or moderation |
| **R-17** | **The spec repeats lorehaven's structure** — a monolith, a ledger nobody reads | medium | high | §24.2's split, with the sections owned by milestones |
| **R-18** | **"The ultimate" becomes three projects in a trench coat** | medium | **high** | one workspace, one domain crate, one schema, one vocabulary, one binary by default; the taste split is a configuration (§8.2) |
| **R-19** | **A feature ships whose machinery is unreachable** — nothing writes the table, nothing enqueues the job | **high** — the estate's most common defect | medium | the reachability probe, the bidirectional route guard, the table-writer audit (§23.5) |
| **R-22** | **The import is 111,686 works, 634,231 tags and 3,891,300 tag assertions**, and the catalogue export, the taxonomy canonicalisation and the taste service's index build all scale with it | **certain** — the numbers are measured | medium | the import is a bulk job with a resumable cursor (§9.8.3); the catalogue export is versioned and content-addressed; and the taste service's ingest was **already measured at this exact scale** — 181.6 MB, 1 m 31 s, on the same 112,935 works. The risk is not the size; it is the taxonomy |
| **R-23** | **97 % of the freeform namespace is singletons**, so the taxonomy is either 634,231 meaningless nodes or 56,197 useful ones, and the choice is invisible until the tree is built | **certain** — measured | medium | the work-count floor (§6.4) makes the choice explicit and configurable, and a tag below the floor stays a searchable, countable assertion rather than disappearing. The floor is a **default with a documented number**, not a heuristic |
| **R-24** | **The fandom is inferred, not filed.** Only 51 of 112,935 works carry a `fandoms` tag, so a name-shape heuristic is doing the work of a taxonomy | high | medium | the heuristic is measured, not assumed; the per-fandom diversity cap is asserted against the *inferred* fandom and the inference is a named, testable function; and a mis-detected fandom degrades a diversity cap, never a filter (§11.7, N-16) |
| **R-25** | **The inherited signal weights are shipped as if they were fitted**, and the recommender's quality is attributed to a design that nobody measured | medium | **high** | the table is labelled inherited and unvalidated in §12.7; M15 fits them by grid search against NDCG@10 on a deterministic hold-out; and M19 cannot ship before M15 |
| **R-21** | **A small group captures a theme's weights**, and the instance's most visible surface drifts toward what a dozen people want | medium — the classic capture attack on a governed shared resource | the owner's 20 % seed floor is permanent; the weight moves at most 25 % of the remaining distance per period; **membership and publication are quorum decisions, not votes**; the applied weight lags the computed weight and both are published; blanket approval flattens but cannot invert (§12.12.8) |
| **R-20** | **The PG-only defect class recurs** | high | medium | both engines gated on every SQL change; the trap scripts; parity tests (§23.3) |
