# Finding stories to read

How to find something good to read, even if you don't know what you're
looking for.

## Browse the front page

Click **Discover**. This shows stories the site thinks are worth your time,
newest activity first. Scroll until something catches your eye — the short
description on each card is written by the author to hook you.

## Search

There is a search box at the top of Discover. Type anything — a title, an
author name, or a word you hope appears in a description — and press Enter.

## Use tags and fandoms

Every story carries labels. Use them:

- Click **any tag** on a story card (for example "slow burn") to see every
  other story with that tag.
- Click a **fandom** name to see everything written in that world.

Tags are the fastest way from "I liked this one story" to "give me fifty
more like it".

## The rating system

Like movie ratings, every story says what's in it: **General**, **Teen**,
**Mature**, or **Explicit**. Your account starts with everything visible.
If you'd rather not see the adult stuff, or want only the adult stuff,
change this in **Account → Content preferences**.

## Save what you like

- **Kudos** (the heart button) tells the author you liked it.
- **Save to Library** puts it on your reading list and remembers your
  place, chapter by chapter.

## Reading comfortably

On any story page, look for the **reader settings** (the "Aa" button):
bigger text, a dark background for night reading, or a font that's easier
on the eyes. Your choice is remembered.

## Nothing looks good?

The **Community** forum has a section where people ask for recommendations.
Post what you've liked before and fans of the same thing will point you at
their favorites. That's what a fan community is for.
