# Getting started

Welcome! This page explains what this site is and how to do the four things
most people come here to do. Every word here assumes you have never used a
fanfiction website before.

## What this site is

This is a place to **read**, **write**, and **talk about** fanfiction —
stories written by fans, set in worlds from books, shows, games, and more.
It is run by a real person (the site owner), not a company. You do not need
an account to read.

## The four things you can do

### 1. Read a story

1. Click **Discover** in the menu at the top.
2. You will see a list of stories. Each card shows the title, a short
   description, and tags (labels like "slow burn" or "fluff" that describe
   the story).
3. Click a title that looks interesting.
4. Read the first chapter. At the bottom of the page there are buttons for
   the next chapter, and a **Library** button that saves your place so you
   can continue later.

That's it. No account needed.

### 2. Save your place (free account)

If you want the site to remember where you stopped reading:

1. Click **Sign in** at the top, then **Create account**.
2. Fill in an email address and a password. That's all.
3. Now the **Library** page remembers every story you saved and which
   chapter you were on.

### 3. Post your own story

1. Sign in, then click **Write** in the menu.
2. Give your story a title and a short summary.
3. Write or paste your first chapter into the big text box. The editor
   works like a normal word processor — select text to make it bold or
   italic, or to add a link.
4. Click **Save**. Your story is a private **draft** — nobody can see it
   but you, and you can come back and edit it any time.
5. When you are happy with it, click **Publish**. Now it appears in
   Discover and other people can read it.

### 4. Talk to people

Click **Community** in the menu. That is the forum: a place to discuss
stories, ask for help with writing, and get to know other fans. Reading the
forum is open to everyone; posting needs an account.

## Words you will see on this site

- **Work** — a story. "Work" is the word this site uses for any piece of
  writing, long or short.
- **Chapter** — one part of a story. Long stories are split into chapters.
- **Tag** — a label describing a story, like "angst" or "enemies to
  lovers". Click a tag to find more stories like it.
- **Fandom** — the world a story is set in, like a book series or a TV
  show.
- **Pseud** — the pen name you post under. You can have more than one, so
  your romance writing and your horror writing can live under different
  names.
- **Kudos** — a quick "I liked this" button on a story. No account needed
  to give one as a reader, and authors can see how many they got.
- **Draft** — a story only you can see, while you are still working on it.
- **Publish** — the button that makes your draft visible to everyone.
