# Your account and privacy

What the site knows about you, and the controls you have.

## What an account is

An account is an email address and a password. Nothing else is required —
no real name, no birthday. Your email is never shown to anyone; the site
uses it only for signing in and for notices you ask for (like password
resets).

## Pseuds: your pen names

You write and post under a **pseud** — a public name you choose. One
account can have several pseuds, which is handy for keeping different
kinds of writing separate (your fluff and your horror don't have to share
a name). Manage them under **Account → Pseuds**.

Your pseuds are not linked to each other publicly. Nobody can see that
two pen names belong to the same account unless you tell them.

## Privacy controls (Account → Privacy)

- **Hide my library** — your reading list is private by default; you can
  make a public "shelf" of favorites if you want one.
- **Hide my reading history** — even from yourself on other devices.
- **Who may comment on my stories** — everyone, only signed-in users, or
  nobody.
- **Who may message me** — everyone, nobody, or only people you follow.

## Blocking

**Account → Blocking**. Blocking an account means:

- You don't see their posts, comments, or forum topics.
- They don't see yours.
- They can't comment on your stories or message you.
- They are **never told**. Blocks are silent.

You can unblock at any time.

## Content filters (Account → Content preferences)

Choose which content ratings you see (General / Teen / Mature / Explicit)
and hide entire fandoms or tags you never want to see. Filters apply to
search, Discover, and recommendations everywhere.

## Deleting your account

**Account → Danger zone → Delete account.** This deletes your pseuds,
drafts, and reading history. Published stories can either be deleted with
the account or left up (you choose). There is a short waiting period in
case you change your mind, then it's permanent.

## What the site owner can see

This site is run by a person, not a company. The owner can see server
logs (which pages were requested) and moderation records. They cannot see
your password (only a scrambled version) or read your private drafts
except when handling a report about them. The moderation log records
every time an owner looks at reported content.
