# Posting your first story

From a blank page to a published story, one step at a time. You need an
account first — see [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).

## Step 1: open the editor

Sign in, then click **Write** in the top menu. Then click **New story**.

## Step 2: the details page

You'll be asked for:

- **Title** — anything you want.
- **Summary** — two or three sentences telling readers what they're in for.
  This is your shop window; a good summary does more for your readership
  than anything else on this page.
- **Rating** — pick honestly. If there's violence, say so. Readers set
  filters and trust them.
- **Fandom** — which world is this set in? Start typing and pick from the
  suggestions.
- **Tags** — labels like "fluff", "hurt/comfort", "one shot". Tags are how
  readers find you.

None of this is locked in — you can change all of it later.

## Step 3: write

The editor works like any word processor: type, select text, use the
buttons for **bold**, *italic*, and links. Your work saves automatically as
you go — look for "Saved" at the top of the page. If you lose your
connection, nothing is lost.

To add another chapter later, open the story and click **Add chapter**.

## Step 4: the draft stage

When you click **Save**, your story is a **draft**. Drafts are private:
only you can see them, and they don't appear in search or Discover. Stay in
draft stage as long as you like — a day or a year.

You can preview exactly what readers will see with the **Preview** button.

## Step 5: publish

When you're ready, click **Publish**. Your story goes live: it appears in
Discover, in tag pages, and in the fandom's page.

Changing your mind is fine:

- **Edit** any time — fix typos, swap chapters, update tags.
- **Withdraw** takes the story back to draft. Readers who saved it keep
  their copies; new readers can't find it.

## What readers can do on your story

- **Kudos** — a one-click "I liked this".
- **Comments** — leave feedback. You can reply, and you can delete any
  comment on your own story.
- **Subscribe** — get notified when you post the next chapter.

## House rules, briefly

- Only post writing you have the right to post. No copying someone else's
  story and calling it yours.
- Rate your content honestly.
- Be kind in comments. The site is positivity-first: constructive
  feedback, no pile-ons.

If you'd like feedback on a draft before publishing, the Community forum
has a critique section — see [The forum, explained](/docs/the-forum-explained).
