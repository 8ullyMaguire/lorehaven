# Importing stories from other sites

If your stories already exist on another fanfiction site, you don't have
to copy them in by hand. The site can fetch them for you.

## What importing does

You give the site a link to your story on the other site. It reads the
chapters, brings them over, and creates a **draft** here with the same
title, summary, chapters, and notes. A draft is private — nothing goes
live until you press Publish. You can fix anything the import got wrong
before anyone sees it.

## Supported sites

The import works with stories posted on the major fanfiction sites (the
list grows; the import page always shows the current one). If your story
is on a site that isn't listed, you can still paste chapters in manually
— see [Posting your first story](/docs/posting-your-first-story).

## How to import

1. Sign in and go to **Import** (under your account menu).
2. Paste the **web address of your story** on the other site — the page
   you'd send a friend who wanted to read it.
3. Click **Fetch**. You'll see a preview: title, chapter list, word
   counts.
4. Check the preview. If it looks right, click **Import**. If a chapter
   looks wrong, untick it — you can add it by hand later.
5. The imported story appears in your drafts. Edit whatever needs fixing,
   then Publish when ready.

## Rules of the road

- **Import your own work, or work you have permission to move.** The
  import preview shows a reminder of this. Importing someone else's story
  and publishing it as yours is theft, and it's the fastest way to get
  your account banned.
- **Check the result.** Automatic imports are good but not perfect.
  Author's notes sometimes land in odd places, and formatting can need a
  touch-up. The draft stage exists exactly so you can review in private.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't find a story at that address"** — check you pasted the
  story's own page, and the whole address.
- **Chapters missing** — some sites hide chapters behind clicks the
  importer can't make. Import the rest by hand.
- **Formatting looks broken** — the draft editor is a normal word
  processor; select the broken part and fix it.

The import page shows the progress of each fetch, and your Import page
keeps a history of past imports.
